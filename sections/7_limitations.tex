\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Independent biological units.}  The coefficient reference
  distribution assumes independent libraries.  FACS bins from one donor are a
  compositional partition, and the HT-29 harvests come from one passaged
  culture.  These designs require a joint-bin, blocked, or repeated-measures
  model for confirmatory inference.

  \item \textbf{Finite-sample dispersion.}  Guide-specific dispersion is
  treated as a plug-in estimate.  With few libraries, its uncertainty is not
  fully propagated by the residual $t$ reference.  Dispersion moderation,
  phenotype permutation, and prespecified negative controls should therefore
  be evaluated together.

  \item \textbf{Calibration separation.}  A control used to estimate a
  calibration scale cannot also provide an unbiased assessment of that scale.
  Held-out controls, cross-fitting, or an independent experiment are required.

  \item \textbf{Likelihood-compatible inputs.}  The Liang values are
  normalized, ComBat-corrected pseudo-counts.  That analysis preserves the
  longitudinal design but cannot validate either count likelihood.  Filtering
  is also permissible only if the supplied denominator remains the unfiltered
  mapped-read total.

  \item \textbf{Guide-to-gene inference.}  BARCS is guide-level.  The
  directional Stouffer summaries used for benchmark compatibility do not model
  dependence or heterogeneous guide quality.  A production gene-level method
  should estimate those components hierarchically.
\end{enumerate}
