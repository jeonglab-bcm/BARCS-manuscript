The Results begin with the nominal-versus-realized error-rate analysis because
calibration is a prerequisite for interpreting any discovery comparison.  We
then state the limited relationship to CB\textsuperscript{2}, report the
processed-count longitudinal and ordered-bin sensitivity analyses, and use
controlled simulations to isolate dispersion and denominator choices.
Endpoint, serial-harvest, copy-number, and external false-discovery audits are
reported in the Supplement.
