The Results are organized around the scope of the generalization.  We first
state what is preserved from CB\textsuperscript{2}, then evaluate longitudinal
time effects, covariate-adjusted ordered phenotypes, and factorial
interactions.  Controlled simulations separate ranking performance from
threshold calibration.  Endpoint and false-discovery audits are reported in
the Supplement because they test backward compatibility and analysis
contracts rather than the principal longitudinal claim.
